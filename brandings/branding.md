# Brand direction

*Working product name: **MoveWise**.*

**MoveWise** is its own logistics product, associated with
**Easily Uncomplicated**. The product should have a distinct name and visual
identity while sharing the parent brand's commitment to clear, approachable
software.

The parent brand's tagline is **“Simple on purpose.”** It may be used to
describe the broader brand, but is not automatically the product's tagline.

## Product identity

Use **MoveWise** as the working product name in product interfaces and
product-specific communications. Show its relationship to Easily
Uncomplicated with a restrained endorsement such as **“An Easily
Uncomplicated product”** where appropriate. Avoid making the parent brand
appear to be the product name.

The logistics product can have its own logo and visual identity, distinct from
the Easily Uncomplicated identity. The two identities should feel compatible
when shown together without requiring the product to copy the parent logo.

## Visual direction

Use this proposed palette for MoveWise:

| Color | Hex | Suggested use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep teal | `#174A50` | Navigation, headings, and primary actions |
| Fresh green | `#39A985` | Highlights and secondary actions |
| Pale mint | `#EAF5F1` | Subtle backgrounds and selected states |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Main surfaces |
| Charcoal | `#263238` | Body text and readable contrast |

Keep layouts uncluttered, with readable typography, clear hierarchy, and
consistent controls. Use dark teal or charcoal for text on light backgrounds,
and verify accessible contrast for text, controls, and focus states. Reserve
fresh green for accents unless its contrast is sufficient for the intended use.

## Product and privacy context

Easily Uncomplicated is a collection of small, focused apps, with a stated
privacy-first direction. MoveWise is a separate product associated with that
brand; the association does not by itself mean it follows the
collection's planned static-hosting model or shares its product architecture.

The logistics prototype currently uses local CSV files and includes
demonstration accounts. Do not promise that its data stays only on the user's
device, that it has no backend, or that it follows the collection's account
and syncing model unless the product's implementation supports those claims.
Consult the repository README for what works today and what remains planned.

## Brand principles

- **Clear:** Make workflows and product capabilities easy to understand.
- **Practical:** Help people manage logistics work without unnecessary
  complexity.
- **Trustworthy:** Communicate data practices accurately and put privacy
  considerations first.
- **Approachable:** Use a warm, calm, helpful voice without hype or
  condescension.
- **Honest:** Clearly distinguish working features from planned capabilities.

Use plain language. Lead with the task the product helps someone do, explain
privacy and data storage concretely, and label planned capabilities clearly.

## Open decisions

- Decide whether the product needs its own tagline.
- Finalize the logo and how the parent-brand endorsement appears alongside it.
- Keep the product's privacy and architecture claims grounded in its actual
  implementation.
