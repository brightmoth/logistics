# MoveWise Logo Ideas

*Working product name: **MoveWise**. An Easily Uncomplicated product.*

This file collects logo directions for MoveWise, built from the brand direction. It does not pick a final logo. It gives you concrete options to compare, plus rules to keep whichever one you choose consistent.

---

## 1. What the logo needs to do

- **Say "logistics" at a glance.** Movement, routes, direction, or deliveries should be readable without explanation.
- **Feel clear and calm.** The brand principles are Clear, Practical, Trustworthy, Approachable, Honest. The logo should look simple and steady, not flashy or hyped.
- **Stand on its own.** MoveWise has its own identity and should not copy the Easily Uncomplicated logo.
- **Sit comfortably next to the parent brand.** The two logos should feel compatible when shown together.
- **Work small.** It must stay legible as a favicon, app icon, and navigation header.

---

## 2. Palette (from brand direction)

| Color | Hex | Role in the logo |
|---|---|---|
| Deep teal | `#174A50` | Primary mark and wordmark colour |
| Fresh green | `#39A985` | Accent only (a single detail, not large areas of text) |
| Pale mint | `#EAF5F1` | Backgrounds, app icon tiles, selected states |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Reversed mark on teal, main surfaces |
| Charcoal | `#263238` | Tagline or endorsement text |

**Contrast note:** Fresh green on white is not strong enough for small text, so keep it to accents and large shapes. Use deep teal or charcoal for anything that has to be read.

## 3. Concept directions

### Concept A: The Route Line
A single continuous line that bends from a start point to an end point, forming a simple "M" shape. A small fresh-green dot marks the destination.

- **Idea:** The route *is* the letter. Movement and the brand initial in one stroke.
- **Colours:** Deep teal line, fresh green end dot.
- **Strengths:** Very simple, scales down well, instantly says "routes".
- **Watch out for:** Can look like a generic graph line if the "M" isn't clear enough.

### Concept B: Pin and Path
A location pin whose tail extends into a short path or arrow, so the pin looks like it is moving rather than standing still.

- **Idea:** Location plus motion.
- **Colours:** Deep teal pin, fresh green path or inner dot.
- **Strengths:** Instantly understood.
- **Watch out for:** Location pins are very common. Needs a twist (the path, the angle) to feel ownable.

### Concept C: The Forward Chevron
Two stacked chevrons pointing right, the lower one in fresh green. Forward motion in the simplest shape possible.

- **Idea:** Progress and direction without needing a vehicle or map.
- **Colours:** Deep teal upper chevron, fresh green lower chevron.
- **Strengths:** Extremely clean, works as a tiny icon.
- **Watch out for:** Close to "fast-forward" or "play" symbols. Keep the angles soft.

### Concept D: Box in Motion
A rounded square (a parcel or crate) with one corner opened into an arrow or a swoosh of motion.

- **Idea:** Goods being moved.
- **Colours:** Deep teal box, fresh green motion accent.
- **Strengths:** Clearly about logistics and deliveries.
- **Watch out for:** Can feel like a generic shipping icon. Keep it minimal and avoid tape lines or extra detail.

### Concept E: Two Points, One Connection
Two circles (origin and destination) joined by a curved line. The circles can double as the humps of an "M" when turned sideways.

- **Idea:** Getting from A to B, reduced to its essentials.
- **Colours:** Teal circles and line, green on the destination circle.
- **Strengths:** Calm and elegant, easy to animate later (a dot travelling along the line).
- **Watch out for:** Looks like a network or connectivity icon if the curve is too symmetrical.

### Concept F: Wordmark with a Moving Letter
Text-only logo. "MoveWise" in a clean, friendly sans-serif, with the "o" replaced by a circle that has a small forward notch or arrow cut into it.

- **Idea:** Lets the name do the work, with one detail suggesting motion.
- **Colours:** "Move" in deep teal, "Wise" in charcoal or teal, the detail in fresh green.
- **Strengths:** Very simple, easy to produce, no icon to get wrong.
- **Watch out for:** Needs a separate icon later for small spaces.

---

## 4. Wordmark guidance

- Use a clean, readable sans-serif with slightly rounded or open shapes for an approachable feel.
- Write the name as one word with a capital M and capital W: **MoveWise**.
- Consider a subtle colour or weight shift between "Move" and "Wise", but keep both parts clearly readable.
- Avoid futuristic, techy, or heavy industrial typefaces. The tone is warm and calm, not hyped.

---

## 5. Parent brand endorsement

Show the relationship with Easily Uncomplicated quietly.

- **Text line:** "An Easily Uncomplicated product" in small charcoal text below or beside the logo.
- **Placement:** Footer, About page, login screen, or the bottom of the logo lockup in some uses. Not the main header.
- **Hierarchy:** The MoveWise logo is always larger and more prominent. The parent brand should never look like the product name.
- **Tagline:** "Simple on purpose." belongs to the parent brand. Do not attach it to the MoveWise logo by default. Whether MoveWise gets its own tagline is still an open decision.

## 6. Logo versions to prepare

| Version | Use |
|---|---|
| Full lockup (icon + wordmark) | Website header, README, documents |
| Stacked lockup (icon above wordmark) | Splash screen, social profiles |
| Icon only | Favicon, app icon, small navigation |
| Wordmark only | Tight horizontal spaces |
| Reversed (white on deep teal) | Dark headers and navigation |
| One-colour (teal or charcoal) | Print, stamps, documents |
| With endorsement line | Footer, login, About |

---

## 7. Testing checklist

- [ ] Readable at 16 × 16 px (favicon)
- [ ] Readable at 32 × 32 px (browser tab, nav icon)
- [ ] Still recognisable in one colour (black or teal only)
- [ ] Works on white, pale mint, and deep teal backgrounds
- [ ] Text meets accessible contrast on every background it is used on
- [ ] Does not resemble the Easily Uncomplicated logo
- [ ] Looks compatible when placed beside the Easily Uncomplicated logo
- [ ] Doesn't imply features or privacy claims the product doesn't yet support

---

## 8. Things to avoid

- Padlocks, shields, or cloud symbols. They imply security or hosting promises that may not match what the product actually does today.
- Gradients, shadows, or 3D effects. They go against the uncluttered direction.
- Copying the parent logo's shapes or its blue and red colours.
- More than two or three colours in the mark.
- Fine detail that disappears at small sizes (tiny wheels, windows, road markings).

---

## 9. Suggested shortlist

If you only explore a few, start with these:

1. **Concept A (Route Line)** for the strongest tie between name, letter, and purpose.
2. **Concept E (Two Points, One Connection)** for the calmest and most distinctive feel.
3. **Concept F (Wordmark)** as the simplest fallback, and a good pairing with an icon from A or E.
